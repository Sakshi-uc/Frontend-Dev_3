The Art of Typography

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. The arrangement of type involves selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths, line-spacing, and letter-spacing.

Font Families Demonstration

This paragraph uses a serif font family. Serif fonts have small decorative strokes (serifs) at the ends of letterforms. They're often considered more traditional and are commonly used in print media.

This paragraph showcases a sans-serif font. Sans-serif fonts lack the decorative strokes and have a more modern, clean appearance. They're widely used for digital content due to their readability on screens.

This text demonstrates a monospace font where each character occupies the same amount of horizontal space. Monospace fonts are perfect for displaying code and technical content.
